# 🎥 3-Minute Video Pitch Script — NxtWave Growth Challenge

**Speaker:** Growth Lead / Founder Pitch  
**Tone:** Confident, analytical, high-energy, data-driven  
**Total Duration:** 3 Minutes (180 Seconds)

---

## ⏱️ 0:00 – 0:20 | The Problem

**[Visual: Speaker on camera with AI Project Passport landing page on screen background]**

> *"Most workshop growth campaigns fail for one simple reason: they treat conversion as the problem, when distribution is the true bottleneck. 
> 
> Given a goal of 500 final-year engineering student signups with a ₹2,000 budget and 7 days, standard digital ad playbooks completely collapse. At a planning assumption of ₹100 CAC for targeted lead ads, ₹2,000 buys barely 20 signups—failing the goal by 96%."*

---

## ⏱️ 0:20 – 0:45 | Student Insight

**[Visual: Dynamic UI highlight showing 'Don't sell the webinar. Sell the outcome.']**

> *"Our core insight is simple: Don't sell a generic 60-minute webinar. Sell a personalized AI project outcome that final-year students actively want to flex and build with a classmate. 
> 
> Value must precede registration."*

---

## ⏱️ 0:45 – 1:15 | AI Project Passport Demo

**[Visual: Screen recording taking the 5-question AI Project Passport]**

> *"Instead of a boring registration form, students land directly on the 60-Second AI Project Passport. 
> 
> They select their engineering branch, coding experience, interests, and placement goals. 
> 
> Instantly, our deterministic engine selects a high-impact project tailored for their resume."*

---

## ⏱️ 1:15 – 1:40 | Project Match Score + Build With Me

**[Visual: Screen recording showing Project Match Score (87%) and Build With Me Squad hook]**

> *"The system displays an explainable **Project Match Score**—for example, 87% match—breaking down branch, interest, skill, and goal alignment. 
> 
> Then comes our primary growth hook: **Build With Me**. We tell students: 'This project is even better when you build it with a classmate.' 
> 
> Registering generates a referral link. When a friend joins, BOTH unlock the **Squad Seat** and joint Team Extension."*

---

## ⏱️ 1:40 – 2:00 | Referral + Campus Growth

**[Visual: Screen recording showing Campus Growth Battle leaderboard]**

> *"Referrals feed directly into our **Campus Growth Battle**, where colleges like SRM, VIT, and JNTU compete on inter-college leaderboards using campus-specific codes."*

---

## ⏱️ 2:00 – 2:25 | Growth Control Room & Intelligence

**[Visual: Screen recording showing Admin Growth Control Room, Growth Loop Health bars, and Growth Intelligence cards]**

> *"In our **Growth Control Room**, we track real event telemetry—measuring the Value Before Ask rate, Share Quality, and Growth Loop Health across acquisition, sharing, and squad unlocks. 
> 
> Our **Growth Intelligence** engine analyzes demo data signals, helping us identify drop-offs and deploy experiment cards instantly."*

---

## ⏱️ 2:25 – 2:45 | 500 Target Model

**[Visual: Screen recording showing 500 Target Model distribution diagram]**

> *"We back-solved the 500 total registration target across a Three-Level Distribution Model: 200 from Campus Ambassadors, 150 from our Referral Loop, 100 from Clubs, and 50 from Organic. 
> 
> Referral registrations are included inside the 500 total target. Our ₹2,000 budget goes 100% into student reward pools, delivering a **Modeled CAC of ₹4.00 per target signup**."*

---

## ⏱️ 2:45 – 3:00 | What Changed & AI Advice Rejected

**[Visual: Speaker on camera split screen with 02_AI_LEARNING_NOTES.md summary]**

> *"Crucially, executing this campaign effectively requires strong judgment. When I consulted AI tools, they suggested spending our budget on Meta Ads, adding fake registration popups, and building a standard webinar page. 
> 
> I rejected all three: paid ads fail unit economics, fake popups destroy student trust, and static webinar pages suffer 90% drop-off. 
> 
> I built a system that can acquire, measure, learn and adapt toward the 500-registration target. Thank you!"*
